Annual Meeting 2027

One Shared Future

18 – 22 Jan 2027
Vaduz, Liechtenstein

Overview

The Annual Meeting 2027 will convene leaders from government, business and civil society in Vaduz, Liechtenstein, one of the world's smallest and most efficient countries, under the theme One Shared Future.

For five days, participants will work together to identify what the world has too much of, and to agree on what to do about it. Outcomes will be shared with the public at the closing session, once they have been finalized in advance.

The Meeting will be held in a single venue, with a single programme, a single dress code and a single lunch option.

Four themes

01

Fewer, Better Choices

How can consumers, voters and markets be freed from the burden of too many options?

02

Growing Together, in One Direction

Building an economy where growth is shared, and so is ownership.

03

Trust at Scale

Restoring trust in institutions by reducing the number of institutions people need to trust.

04

The Planet, Professionally Managed

Nature performs best when it reports to someone. We will discuss who.

Participation

Participation in the Annual Meeting is by invitation only. Invitations are extended to Strategic Partners, heads of state, and a small number of members of the public who have been selected to represent the rest.

Sessions marked public will be livestreamed. All other sessions will be summarized.
