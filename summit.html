WorldCon Summit

Our annual summit where leaders meet.

Once a year, WorldCorp International convenes a closed-door gathering of executives, operators, and innovators. Attendance is by invitation. Join our mailing list to be considered for the waitlist.

About the Summit

What

A multi-day gathering where leadership is discussed, by leaders, in front of other leaders, with breaks for coffee.

Who

A curated group of decision-makers, several of whom will describe themselves afterward as “energized.”

Why

Because the calendar called for it, and because last year’s attendees asked when the next one was.

Outcomes

Attendees leave with a printed agenda, a badge, and a renewed sense of where the industry is headed, roughly.

Quick Facts
Dates
Third week of October
Location
A city with an airport
Format
In-person, hybrid option available
Attendance
By invitation
Dress Code
Business, loosely enforced

Program

Four tracks. One hallway.

Strategy

Where the organization is going, presented as though it were already decided.

Operations

How the organization runs, as understood by people several layers removed from running it.

Narrative

How the organization is described, and why the description keeps changing.

Governance

Who is accountable, in the event that becomes necessary to determine.

Seats are limited.
So is the agenda.
