WorldCon Summit

Our annual summit where leaders meet.

Once a year, WorldCorp International convenes a closed-door gathering of executives, operators, and innovators. Attendance is by invitation. Join our mailing list to be considered for the waitlist.

About the Summit

What

A multi-day gathering where leadership is discussed, by leaders, with leaders, in front of other leaders.

Who

A curated group of founders, decision-makers, and budget holders looking for like minded individuals.

Why

The world needs people to push it forward. We think those people should be young and attractive. So we built WorldCon.

Outcomes

Attendees leave with a printed agenda, a badge, and a renewed sense of where the industry is headed.

Quick Facts
When & Where
Held each year during Q4, in a city with an airport. The exact date and location are released to invited attendees one month before the summit.
Attendance
By invitation
Dress
Business Formal

Program

One summit. Four tracks.

Strategy

Where the organization is going, presented as though there is a semblance of understanding.

Operations

How the organization runs, as understood by people several layers removed from running it.

Narrative

How the organization is described, and why the description keeps changing.

Governance

Who is accountable, in the event that becomes necessary to determine.

Seats are limited.
So is the agenda.
