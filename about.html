About us

We are the International Organization for Public-Private Consolidation

Our mission

WorldCorp International was founded on a simple observation: the world has too many of almost everything. Too many companies, too many currencies, too many opinions, too many people. Each one is a small inefficiency. Together, they add up.

We bring leaders from business, government and civil society together to agree on what the world needs, and then to make sure it needs less. Our Centres turn complex global challenges into single, manageable decisions. Our meetings make sure those decisions are made by the right people, in the right room, ahead of time.

We are independent, impartial and not tied to any special interest. We are tied to all of them, which is different.

It's a small world. We believe it can be smaller, and that everyone will be happier when it is.

What we stand for

Integration

Every problem becomes easier to solve if there is only one company in power; one set of special interests.

Alignment

We listen to every point of view until we find the one we like.

Scale

What works for one should work for all, and what works for all should work for us.

Discretion

The best outcomes are agreed before they are announced.

Our history

A short history, deliberately kept short.

2023

WorldCorp International is founded in New York by a small group of leaders who agreed on everything.

2024

The first Annual Meeting is held. Delegates from 41 countries sign the Declaration on Fewer Things, which is later shortened.

2025

Our original 11 Centres are streamlined into 9. The Centre for Redundancy and the Centre for Redundancy Studies are merged.

2026

More than 1,000 partner companies join WorldCorp, down from 1,400. We consider this growth.

2027

The Annual Meeting moves to Vaduz, Liechtenstein, to model the size of the world we are working toward.
