About The Firm

We exist because, frankly, someone had to.

WorldCorp International is a global strategy, technology, and vibes consultancy headquartered in a group chat that got out of hand. We were founded on the principle that if you say “synergy” with enough confidence, someone will eventually pay you for it.

Our Story

2019

Founded in a group chat, originally as a joke, later as an LLC, currently as neither.

2021

Acquired ourselves in a hostile takeover. Negotiations with ourselves were tense.

2023

Pivoted to blockchain. Un-pivoted from blockchain eleven days later.

2024

Named “Most Confident Consultancy” by an award we invented and gave to ourselves.

2026

Currently disrupting your patience, one billable hour at a time.
