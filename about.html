About The Firm

Business is our business.

WorldCorp International is a global think-tank, market research, and strategy firm. We exist to push the frontier of the working world.

Our Story

2019

Our founder saw somebody smile, and then laugh, in a coffee shop, and thought: that doesn’t feel quite right. Why is somebody expressing themselves? Even worse, why isn’t that expression one of corporate angst and dejection?

2021

Our founder spent two years amassing an enormous amount of capital by innovative, quasi-legal means involving young people.

2023

WorldCorp International was launched with the goal of making the world more corporate.

2024

We closed our first contract.

2026

We have successfully disrupted more positive emotions than any other organization on earth.

Gabe Nipote

Founder & Managing Director

Gabe founded WorldCorp International to help organizations communicate more clearly about what they do and why it matters. He works closely with clients across strategy, marketing, and content.

Amanda Spaggione

Chief Strategy Officer

Amanda sets WorldCorp’s strategic direction, helping clients clarify their positioning before it reaches the market. Prior to WorldCorp, she worked in brand and organizational communications.

Erik Nildgren

VP of Finance

Erik oversees WorldCorp’s financial planning and reporting, translating internal performance into terms that hold up externally. Prior to WorldCorp, he worked in corporate finance.

Garrett Samuels

Head of Content

Garrett leads WorldCorp’s content practice, from long-form documentary work to short-form social, focused on formats that hold an audience’s attention.

Kyle Baum

Director, Client Partnerships

Nora Skay
