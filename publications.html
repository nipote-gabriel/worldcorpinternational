Our methodology

Every WorldCorp report begins with a survey of leaders from business, government and civil society. Responses are weighted by the size of the respondent's organization, and then by its membership tier.

Findings are reviewed by our Strategic Partners for accuracy and alignment. Where findings and alignment disagree, we publish alignment.
